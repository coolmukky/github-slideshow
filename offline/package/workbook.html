Your team’s answer sheet. For each use case, sketch your design and record how you address the customer’s pain points and requirements, and which Cisco products you’d use and why. Your proctor scores each use case out of 100.

How you’ll be scored. Each use case = 100 points: Mapping 40 (pains & requirements) · Products 30 (right products, how & why) · Diagram 30. Use cases 1–3 are required; 4–5 are bonus. Levels: Associate 60–74 · Professional 75–89 · Expert 90–100.
