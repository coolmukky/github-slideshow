Your 7 steps

  • Understand the challenge. Read the Workbook for this use case: the customer's pain points and desired outcomes.
  • Agree your Cisco solution. As a group, discuss and write in the Workbook the Cisco solution that addresses those needs.
  • Draw it on the flip chart. Sketch the high-level logical diagram of your recommended solution.
  • Repeat for every use case. Work through each use case, growing the same diagram as you go.
  • Review with your SME. Walk your SME through the design so far and take their guidance.
  • Digitize with Cisco Circuit. Convert the whiteboard diagram with Circuit; attach the output into the Workbook.
  • Submit for evaluation. Do a final review with your SME, then submit your Workbook.
