Proctor Evaluation Cheat Sheet

Quick at-the-table scoring aid — grade each team fast against these anchors. Full reference answers are in the ZTA Proctor Guide. Do not show participants.

How to score. Each use case = 100 pts: Pain→product mapping 40, Products how & why 30, Diagram & solution 30. Tick the boxes a team hits, award partial credit generously where intent matches, then total. 1–3 required, 4–5 bonus.

Use Case 1 — Managed Environment — Segmentation

Required 100 pts

① Pain-point → product mapping · 40 pts ~8 pts / pain point

  • VLAN/subnet segmentation is too complex → propose SGTs (TrustSec) instead of VLAN/IP.
  • Profiling can’t keep up with new devices → Cisco ISE (cloud AI) profiling.
  • Failing PCI audits → SGT segmentation + insert FW (FTD) services where L7 is required.
  • Corporate PCs behind on AV / OS → ISE Posture checks + remediation before full access.
  • No budget for more firewalls → SGT / SGACL enforcement on the existing switches (no new FW).

② Products — how & why / differentiators · 30 pts

  • Names a specific product with a correct one-line “how it works”.
  • Gives a genuine “why / differentiator”, not a generic benefit.
  • Top band cites Cisco differentiators: SGT/TrustSec (topology-independent), pxGrid / Rapid Threat Containment, ISE AI profiling, Secure Workload (App Dependency Mapping).

Expected set: ISE · ISE-SGT · ISE SGACL policy · ISE pxGrid · Catalyst SD-WAN · FMC / SCC / FTD · Cisco Secure Workload

③ Diagram & overall solution · 30 pts

  • Legible; labels all four groups: Employees, IOT, deskagents, Guest.
  • Marks the enforcement point(s) — SGACL on switches/routers; FW (FTD) where L7 is needed.
  • Traces at least one flow end-to-end.
  • Honors access rules: IoT → data-center + Internet only (blocked from other groups); Guest → Internet only; deskagents → on-prem DC only; Employees + IoT → controlled Internet + DC apps.
  • Reuses the existing network — no wave of new firewalls.

Common gaps / red flags — dock marks

  • Relies on VLANs / IP ACLs for segmentation.
  • Buys new firewalls just to segment (ignores the budget pain).
  • No posture story for the AV / OS-hygiene pain point.
  • Guest not restricted to Internet-only, or IoT not restricted from other groups.
